## Dataset vocabulary (counted 2026-11-02, `kb/raw/exercises.json`)
- 876 exercises. Muscle names (primary and secondary): abdominals, abductors, adductors, biceps, calves, chest, forearms, glutes, hamstrings, lats, lower back, middle back, neck, quadriceps, shoulders, traps, triceps. **`shoulders` is a single group; there is no front/rear split**, so the baseline for shoulders cannot tell rear delts from front delts.
- Level: beginner 525, intermediate 294, **expert 57** (mapped to our `advanced`).
- Equipment: body only 111, machine 67, other 122, foam roll 11, **none (null) 77**, kettlebells 56, dumbbell 123, cable 81, barbell 170, bands 20, medicine ball 17, exercise ball 12, e-z curl bar 9. `other` is ambiguous.
- Category: strength 584, stretching 123, plyometrics 61, strongman 21, powerlifting 38, cardio 14, olympic weightlifting 35. The planner's default pool is `strength` + `powerlifting` (622 chunks); the rest stay indexed for explicit filters.
- Secondary muscles are common (for example shoulders 210, glutes 220), which is why the baseline counting rule (primary 1 set, secondary 0.5) matters.
- Community-written; quality is uneven and not hand-filtered.
