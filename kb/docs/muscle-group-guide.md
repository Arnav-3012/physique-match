# Which muscle groups the plan trains for each body region

The body regions in the gap report map to the muscle groups of the exercise library this app uses. Every group the plan tracks gets at least the typical weekly minimum (about 4 fractional sets, see the volume document), whatever the gap result for its region says, including regions that could not be measured.

- **Shoulders:** the shoulders group. The library has one shoulders group, so front, side and rear shoulder work all count toward it. Chest and triceps exercises also give it some credit.
- **Chest:** the chest group.
- **Upper back:** the middle back and traps groups. Upper-back size is never measured by this app, but the region still gets its weekly minimum.
- **Back width (lats):** the lats group. The app shows this as an estimate; it still gets its weekly minimum even when it could not be measured.
- **Waist:** the abdominals group. Training the abdominal muscles builds that muscle. It does not shrink waist girth, which follows overall body fat.
- **Hips:** the glutes and abductors groups. Few exercises list the abductors as their main muscle, so most of their sets come from exercises where they help.
- **Upper arm:** the biceps and triceps, each with its own minimum.
- **Forearm:** the forearms group.
- **Thigh:** the quadriceps, hamstrings and adductors, each with its own minimum. Adductors have few exercises of their own and mostly get credit from exercises where they help.
- **Calf:** the calves group.

The lower back and neck are not tracked and not prescribed directly. The plan does not forbid compound lifts that also use them.

A set counts 1 for each group an exercise lists as primary and 0.5 for each it lists as secondary. That is an approximation, because the library's lists were not written with this rule.
