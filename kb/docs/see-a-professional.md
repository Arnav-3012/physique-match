# When to talk to a professional

This app gives general information from research. It is not medical advice and it cannot assess your health. Talk to a doctor, physiotherapist, dietitian or qualified coach before you start or change a programme if any of these apply:

- You have a medical condition, are pregnant, or take medication that could affect exercise or eating.
- You have pain, an injury or an old injury, or you feel pain during an exercise. Stop that exercise and get it checked.
- You feel dizzy, faint, chest pain or unusual shortness of breath while training.
- You are under 18. Growth and nutrition needs differ, and the calorie guidance in this app is for adults.
- You have, or have had, a difficult relationship with food or your body image, or you feel distress about how your body looks. A photo comparison can make this worse. Support is available from a doctor or a mental health professional.
- You want to lose or gain weight faster than the typical ranges described in this app.

A coach can also check your exercise technique in person, which no app can do.
