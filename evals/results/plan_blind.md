## Plan A

Summary: A balanced routine targets all major muscle groups across two sessions, blending strength and core work. Each day includes a variety of movements to build stability and muscle tone. Stay consistent and focus on form for the best results.

Day One
- Thigh Abductor: 3 x 8-12. Sit tall, press your legs outward with control.
- Thigh Adductor: 3 x 8-12. Sit upright, squeeze your inner thighs together.
- Standing Leg Curl: 3 x 8-12. Keep hips stable as you curl your heel toward glutes.
- Smith Machine Upright Row: 3 x 8-12. Pull the bar up toward your chest, elbows high.
- Barbell Seated Calf Raise: 3 x 8-12. Press through the balls of your feet, extending your calves.
- Palms-Down Wrist Curl Over A Bench: 3 x 8-12. Lower the bar slowly, then curl your wrists upward.
- Plie Dumbbell Squat: 3 x 8-12. Feet wide, toes out, squat deep while keeping chest up.
- Dips - Triceps Version: 3 x 8-12. Lower yourself until elbows bend, then push up using triceps.
- Rope Straight-Arm Pulldown: 3 x 8-12. Keep arms straight, pull rope down toward hips.

Day Two

- Crunch - Legs On Exercise Ball: 3 x 8-12. Engage core, lift shoulders off the floor.
- Push-Ups With Feet Elevated: 3 x 8-12. Maintain a straight line, lower chest, then press up.
